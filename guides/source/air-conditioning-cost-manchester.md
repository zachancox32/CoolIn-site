These are CoolIn's own installed prices for Manchester and the North West, not national averages. Each one includes the equipment, the fitting, the electrical connection and commissioning, so you can compare them like for like with any quote you get.

## How much does air conditioning cost for one room?

A single wall mounted split, with one indoor unit and one outdoor unit, is the most common installation. The size you need depends mainly on the size of the room.

| Unit size | Usually suits | Installed price |
|---|---|---|
| 2.5 kW | Rooms up to about 15m², such as a bedroom or study | £1,650 |
| 3.5 kW | Rooms up to about 22m², such as a double bedroom or small lounge | £1,995 |
| 5.0 kW | Rooms up to about 32m², such as a large lounge or kitchen diner | £2,280 |
| 7.1 kW | Rooms up to about 45m², such as an open plan ground floor | £2,950 |

South facing glass, a room in the roof or a high ceiling can push a room up a size. The [size calculator](/air-conditioning-size-calculator) works out a starting figure for any room in under a minute.

## How much does it cost to air condition a whole house?

For several rooms, a multi split runs two to five indoor units from a single outdoor unit, so there is only one unit outside the house. Each room has its own controller.

| Rooms | Installed price |
|---|---|
| Two rooms | £3,450 |
| Three rooms | £4,995 |
| Four rooms | £6,650 |
| Five rooms | £8,200 |

A multi split is often the only option on a terraced house, because permitted development allows one outdoor unit on a house that is not detached. The [terraced house guide](/guides/air-conditioning-terraced-house) explains how that works.

## What do cassettes, floor consoles and ducted systems cost?

| System | Installed price |
|---|---|
| Floor console, 2.5 kW | £2,150 |
| Ceiling cassette, 3.5 kW | £2,450 |
| Ducted, two rooms | £3,500 |

A floor console suits a room with no space high on the wall, such as a loft conversion. A ducted system hides the indoor unit in a loft or ceiling void, so the only thing on show in each room is a grille.

## What is included in the price?

- Indoor and outdoor units, matched as a pair
- Wall brackets or floor feet, and anti vibration mounts
- Up to five metres of insulated pipe run in white trunking
- Core drilling through the wall, made good, and a condensate drain
- The electrical connection, an isolator and testing
- Vacuum testing, commissioning and the handover documents
- The manufacturer's warranty registered in your name
- Your first annual service, free

## What makes air conditioning cost more?

- Pipe runs over five metres, or routes through more than one floor
- Scaffolding or a tower where access is awkward
- An outdoor unit on a flat roof, on a bracket at height or in a lightwell
- Chasing the pipework into the walls rather than running it in trunking
- A new circuit from the consumer unit
- Design ranges, colour finishes or designer fronts
- Listed buildings and conservation areas, where consent is needed

In Manchester the biggest variable is usually the pipe route. A terraced house with the outdoor unit in the rear yard often keeps it short. A city centre apartment can need longer runs, access equipment and the freeholder's consent, which the [guide to flats and apartments](/guides/air-conditioning-flat-or-apartment) covers.

## Does air conditioning carry VAT?

Not on a domestic installation until 31 March 2027. Energy saving materials fitted in homes are zero rated, and HMRC treats most air conditioning units as air source heat pumps because they heat as well as cool. The relief only applies when the same company supplies and installs the system.

From 1 April 2027 the rate is set to return to 5%. Commercial installations carry VAT at the standard rate, which a VAT registered business can normally reclaim. Your quote sets out the VAT position in writing.

## How much does air conditioning cost to run?

Less than most people expect. At an electricity price of 25p per unit, a 2.5kW bedroom unit costs:

| Running state | Power drawn | Cost per hour |
|---|---|---|
| Holding the temperature | about 0.3 kW | about 7p |
| Working hard on a hot afternoon | about 0.65 kW | about 16p |

The full working, including heating costs and the comparison with a fan heater, is in the [running costs article](/blog/air-conditioning-running-costs).

## Why are national price guides different?

National price guides average across the whole country and every type of installer, and they do not all include the same things. Some quote the equipment alone, some leave out the electrical connection or the pipe run, and some show prices before VAT.

To compare quotes fairly, check that each one includes the same unit size and brand, the pipe run length, the electrical connection, commissioning, warranty registration and the VAT position. A cheaper quote that leaves one of those out is not cheaper.
