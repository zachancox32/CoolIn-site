How much does air conditioning cost in Manchester?

Reviewed October 2026 by Zac Hancox, Founder of CoolIn

The short answer

A single room costs from £1,650 fitted for a 2.5kW wall unit, and two rooms on one outdoor unit from £3,450. Domestic installations carry no VAT until 31 March 2027. These are installed prices covering the units, up to five metres of pipe, the electrical connection, commissioning and your first annual service.

  • One room from £1,650 fitted, for a 2.5kW wall unit
  • Two to five rooms on one outdoor unit from £3,450
  • No VAT on domestic installations until 31 March 2027
  • Up to five metres of pipe, the electrics and commissioning included
  • Running a bedroom unit costs roughly 7p to 16p an hour
  • Servicing from £89 per indoor unit per year

These are CoolIn's own installed prices for Manchester and the North West, not national averages. Each one includes the equipment, the fitting, the electrical connection and commissioning, so you can compare them like for like with any quote you get.

What is included in the price?

  • Indoor and outdoor units, matched as a pair
  • Wall brackets or floor feet, and anti vibration mounts
  • Up to five metres of insulated pipe run in white trunking
  • Core drilling through the wall, made good, and a condensate drain
  • The electrical connection, an isolator and testing
  • Vacuum testing, commissioning and the handover documents
  • The manufacturer's warranty registered in your name
  • Your first annual service, free

What makes air conditioning cost more?

  • Pipe runs over five metres, or routes through more than one floor
  • Scaffolding or a tower where access is awkward
  • An outdoor unit on a flat roof, on a bracket at height or in a lightwell
  • Chasing the pipework into the walls rather than running it in trunking
  • A new circuit from the consumer unit
  • Design ranges, colour finishes or designer fronts
  • Listed buildings and conservation areas, where consent is needed

In Manchester the biggest variable is usually the pipe route. A terraced house with the outdoor unit in the rear yard often keeps it short. A city centre apartment can need longer runs, access equipment and the freeholder's consent, which the guide to flats and apartments covers.

Does air conditioning carry VAT?

Not on a domestic installation until 31 March 2027. Energy saving materials fitted in homes are zero rated, and HMRC treats most air conditioning units as air source heat pumps because they heat as well as cool. The relief only applies when the same company supplies and installs the system.

From 1 April 2027 the rate is set to return to 5%. Commercial installations carry VAT at the standard rate, which a VAT registered business can normally reclaim. Your quote sets out the VAT position in writing.

Why are national price guides different?

National price guides average across the whole country and every type of installer, and they do not all include the same things. Some quote the equipment alone, some leave out the electrical connection or the pipe run, and some show prices before VAT.

To compare quotes fairly, check that each one includes the same unit size and brand, the pipe run length, the electrical connection, commissioning, warranty registration and the VAT position. A cheaper quote that leaves one of those out is not cheaper.

Questions people ask next

Why do air conditioning prices online vary so much?

National price guides average across very different jobs, regions and installers, and they do not all include the same things. Some quote the unit alone, some leave out the electrical connection, the pipe run or VAT. To compare fairly, check each quote includes the same unit size, pipe run, electrical connection, commissioning, warranty registration and VAT position.

Is it cheaper to buy the unit online and pay someone to fit it?

Usually not. The 0% VAT relief only applies when the same company supplies and installs the system, so a unit bought separately carries 20% VAT. Many installers will not fit equipment they did not supply, and the manufacturer's extended warranty often depends on the installer registering the system.

Do you charge for the survey?

No. The survey is free, usually takes under an hour, and produces a fixed written price within 48 hours. That price does not change once you accept it.

Will the price go up after the survey?

No. The guide prices are a realistic starting point for a straightforward job. The survey looks at the things that move the price, such as the pipe route, access and where the outdoor unit can go, and the written price it produces is fixed once you accept it.

How much does commercial air conditioning cost?

A single 5kW wall unit in a small shop or office starts at £2,280 installed, a four cassette system for an office floor is around £8,500, and a six unit VRF system around £14,000. Commercial work carries VAT at the standard rate, which a VAT registered business can normally reclaim.
