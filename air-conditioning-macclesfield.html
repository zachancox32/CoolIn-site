Local knowledge

What the buildings in Macclesfield tend to need

Macclesfield has an unusual concentration of listed and locally significant buildings for a town its size, and the eastern side sits high enough to feel a genuinely different winter.

Listed and silk era buildings

No permitted development rights, so consent is needed before anything is fixed to the structure. We prepare the application with positions, noise data and photographs, and speak to the conservation officer before quoting rather than after.

Prestbury and the larger houses

Whole house systems where the priority is keeping the number of visible outdoor units to a minimum and the pipework entirely out of sight.

Rising ground to the east

Bollington and the lanes climbing towards the moors sit noticeably higher and colder than the town centre. Heating performance needs explaining honestly at those addresses.

Town centre offices and clinics

Converted upper floors with awkward access and shared structures, where slim ducted units above a false ceiling usually beat a wall unit both visually and for the landlord.

Local questions

Asked most often in Macclesfield

Can air conditioning go into a listed building in Macclesfield?

Often, but it needs listed building consent because permitted development rights do not apply at all. What tends to get approved is a condenser in a rear courtyard or another position not visible from a public highway, with indoor units chosen to avoid drilling through historic fabric where there is any alternative. We put the application together and have the conversation with the conservation officer before you commit to anything.

Is it worth having heating from it this side of the Peak District?

Yes, for most of the year. Between roughly 7°C and 15°C outside, which covers a large part of a Cheshire autumn and spring, an air to air heat pump heats a room far more cheaply than firing a boiler to warm the whole house. On the genuinely cold weeks it gives less, and above 200 metres you get more of those weeks. We are clear about where the line falls for your specific address.
