Local knowledge

What the buildings in Altrincham tend to need

Altrincham splits neatly into three jobs: big period houses, modern rear extensions bolted onto them, and a food and drink scene squeezed into small units.

Bowdon and Hale villas

Substantial detached houses with high ceilings, meaning you are cooling volume rather than floor area. Multi split systems running four or five rooms from one condenser keep a single unit on the elevation rather than a row of them.

Glass extensions and orangeries

The most common call we get here. A south facing rear extension with bifolds and a lantern roof gains heat faster than almost any other room in a house, and needs sizing on glazing area rather than floor size.

Bowdon conservation area

Restricts where the outdoor unit can sit: it must not be nearer a bounding highway than the house itself. That usually means a rear or side position, agreed at survey before anything is quoted.

Town centre hospitality

Altrincham Market and the bars around it run small kitchens in tight spaces. Extract with matched make up air, fitted overnight, so the doors open as normal the next day.

Local questions

Asked most often in Altrincham

Do I need permission for air conditioning in the Bowdon conservation area?

Permitted development still applies, with an extra restriction: the outdoor unit must not sit nearer to a highway bounding the property than any part of the house. In practice that pushes it to a rear or side elevation, which is usually where you would want it anyway. Listed buildings, of which Bowdon has several, have no permitted development rights at all and need consent. We check the designation at survey and tell you before you commit.

What size unit does a glass extension need?

More than the floor area suggests. A 20m2 extension with bifolds and a roof lantern can need 5kW where the same 20m2 of ordinary bedroom would take 3.5kW. We size on the glazed area and its orientation, because on a south or west facing extension the glass is doing most of the heating, not the room.
