Common questions

The things people ask on the first call

More detail on the domestic and commercial pages, where the questions get more specific.

How long does an installation take?

A single room is usually one day. A three or four room multi split is two days. Commercial projects depend on the number of units and the access, and we give you a day by day plan with the quote.

Does it need servicing every year?

Yes, and the manufacturers require it to keep the warranty running. A service is a filter clean, a coil clean, a gas pressure check and a drain test. Our plans start at £89 per unit and include priority callout.

Which areas do you cover?

Roughly an hour from our base in Manchester, which takes in Greater Manchester, most of Cheshire, Merseyside and southern Lancashire. The full list is further down this page. If you are just outside it, ring us anyway and we will either come to you or point you at someone who can.

What standard is the work held to?

Nobody opens a refrigerant circuit on your system without current F-Gas certification. That is a legal requirement for handling refrigerant rather than a badge, and we will show you the certificate if you want to see it. Electrical work is notifiable under Part P and is certified on completion.

CoolIn holds the contract throughout. We invoice you, our own workmanship is guaranteed for two years on top of the manufacturer warranty, and if something needs putting right you ring us rather than chasing anyone else.
