Local knowledge

What the buildings in Salford tend to need

Most Salford enquiries are apartments, and most apartments come with a freeholder, a managing agent and a lease that has something to say about the outside of the building.

Quays apartments

Floor to ceiling glazing facing water, with no shading and windows that open a few inches at most. These flats have no way of shedding heat, which is why they are among the most uncomfortable homes in the region by July.

Freeholder and management consent

Nearly every apartment job needs approval before anything is fixed outside. We prepare the request with unit positions, noise figures and photographs, which is the difference between an approval and a refusal.

Systems with no outdoor unit

Where a condenser genuinely cannot be fixed to the building, self contained units that vent through a small core hole are the fallback. They are less efficient and noisier than a split, and we will say so, but they work where nothing else is permitted.

MediaCity offices and studios

Edit suites and equipment rooms with steady year round heat loads, and open plan floors that need zoning. Out of hours fitting so broadcast and production are never interrupted.

Local questions

Asked most often in Salford

Can I get air conditioning in a Salford Quays apartment?

Usually, but the lease decides. Almost all of them require the freeholder or managing agent to consent to anything fixed to the exterior, and the permitted development limits for flats are tighter than for houses. Where a wall mounted condenser is refused, the options are a balcony position if the lease allows it, a shared plant location agreed with the building manager, or a self contained unit with no outdoor part. We work out which applies at survey rather than after you have paid.

Why are apartments so much hotter than houses?

Three reasons stacked on top of each other. Large areas of glazing with no shading, windows restricted to a few inches for safety so there is almost no ventilation, and heat arriving from the flats around and below you rather than only from outside. A top floor flat with a west facing wall of glass can be twelve degrees above the outside temperature by evening.
