What the buildings in Lancaster tend to need

Local questions

Asked most often in Lancaster

Are you too far away to look after a system in Lancaster?

For installation, no. It is a planned visit and 75 minutes each way is easily programmed. For an emergency callout in the middle of a heatwave, it is honestly the weakest part of our offer, and we would rather say that now than after you have paid. Service plan customers get next working day attendance, which we hold to. Without a plan, at that distance, you may wait. If reliable same week response matters more than anything, a Lancaster based installer may serve you better and we will say so.

Does Morecambe need the coastal specification too?

Yes. Anywhere within roughly a mile of open water, salt in the air corrodes the aluminium fins on a standard outdoor coil and can take years off the condenser. Coated coil versions of the same units cost a modest premium and are what we fit as standard on those addresses.
