Good to know

Questions people often have at this point

  • Is now a good time to install?Winter means no summer rush, and home installations carry no VAT until 31 March 2027.
  • Do you live in a flat?The lease usually decides more than planning does. What the freeholder will ask for.
  • Is it a terraced house?Where the outdoor unit can go, and the rules on noise and the front of the house.
  • Not sure which brand?Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric, Samsung and the rest, compared plainly.
  • What will it cost to run?Pence per hour for cooling and heating, with the sums shown.
