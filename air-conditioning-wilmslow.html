Local knowledge

What the buildings in Wilmslow tend to need

Wilmslow work tends to be whole house rather than single room, and the finish matters as much as the performance.

Whole house multi splits

Four or five bedrooms plus a study, all running from one or two outdoor units. Getting the condenser count down is usually the design priority, which means sizing carefully so the outdoor unit is not the limiting factor on a hot night.

Discreet installation

Slim ducted units above a landing ceiling, with only a linear grille visible, are common here. They cost more than a wall unit and they are the difference between a system you notice and one you do not.

Home offices and annexes

Converted garages, garden rooms and above garage offices are rarely on the main heating system and run cold in winter and hot in summer. A single unit fixes both ends of that, cheaply.

Pools, gyms and cinema rooms

High heat loads in sealed rooms. These need ventilation designed alongside the cooling, because recirculating the same air harder does nothing for humidity or air quality.

Local questions

Asked most often in Wilmslow

Can you install air conditioning without it being visible from the road?

Almost always. The indoor side can be slim ducted so only a grille shows, and the outdoor unit can go on a side or rear elevation, in a screened enclosure or behind planting, as long as it keeps clear airflow of roughly 300mm behind and a metre in front. Boxing a condenser in tightly to hide it is the one thing that genuinely hurts performance, so we design the screen rather than bolting one on afterwards.

Will one outdoor unit run five rooms?

It can, but the outdoor unit caps the total. Five 2.5kW indoor units on a 10kW condenser gives you 10kW shared between them when everything runs at once, not 12.5kW. In a house where all five rooms are occupied on the same hot evening that matters, so we size the condenser against how you actually use the house rather than the theoretical maximum.
