Local knowledge

What the buildings in Bolton tend to need

Housing stock and building type change what a good installation looks like far more than the badge on the unit does.

Stone terraces

Thick walls hold temperature well once they get there, which cuts both ways: slower to warm and slower to overheat. Core drilling takes longer and we allow for it rather than discovering it on the day.

Mill and industrial conversions

High ceilings, big volumes and heat that collects out of reach. Spot cooling over the occupied areas usually beats trying to condition the whole space, and destratification fans bring the warm air back down in winter.

Trade counters and workshops

Heavy duty units, good filtration for a dusty environment, and positions well clear of forklift routes.

Family homes

Multi splits covering two or three bedrooms from a single condenser, which keeps one box on the wall instead of three.

Local questions

Asked most often in Bolton

Can you cool a workshop or industrial unit?

Yes, though the approach differs from an office. Trying to cool the entire volume of a high bay unit is rarely economic. Spot cooling over workstations and occupied areas gets the people comfortable for a fraction of the running cost, and destratification fans push the warm air back down in winter instead of letting it sit in the roof.

How quickly can you get to Bolton for a breakdown?

Bolton is around 30 minutes from our Manchester base. Service plan customers get same day phone support and next working day attendance. Without a plan you go behind contract customers, which through July and August can mean several days.
